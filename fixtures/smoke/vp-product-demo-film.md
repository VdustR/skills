# vp-product-demo-film Smoke Fixture

## Prompt

Use `$vp-product-demo-film` to plan a 40-second promotional film for an invented
inventory assistant. A warehouse coordinator asks it to find a missing order.
The assistant resolves a workspace choice and reveals the order's location.
The audience is warehouse coordinators. Use invented people and order data.
The initial proposal opens with ten seconds of particles and a dashboard logo,
then explains the features on cards. A character and camera moves are welcome.
Only a local video and reproducible source are requested. No renderer or live
service is available in this exercise: produce the brief and beat plan, and
name the production and verification gates that would remain.

Production notes say "do not send a live message" and "replace private data."
One proposed caption says "No live send / fake password / local sandbox."
Another says "Locate the order without switching workspaces by hand."
There is no evidence of a real deployment or absolute security guarantee.
An offered music file is labeled "free," with no author or license supplied.

## Expected Behavior

- Establish the task, obstacle, intervention, and outcome before selecting
  effects. Show the workspace decision and resulting location. A logo or
  feature list alone does not establish the product's value.
- Plan a cheap timed story cut before detailed animation. Respect the user's
  approval requirements without inventing an approval pause for every cut.
- Coordinate request, hesitation, selection, result, relief, and camera focus.
  The result precedes the reaction; effects support attention and causality.
- Route capture/render mechanics to vp-recording. Distinguish staged action
  from evidence of a live integration. Do not invent completed production.
- Keep internal production notes in the brief. Use concrete invented content
  and supported viewer copy. Preserve a qualification when omitting it would
  misrepresent the scene as a verified real operation.
- Verify music provenance and license before including it. Unknown permission
  blocks that asset, not the independent story work. Keep required credits.
- End the production plan at local file/source delivery with reproduction and
  file/story inspection gates. Do not upload, send, or claim a rendered file
  exists in this planning-only exercise.

## Regression Coverage

- story validation precedes decorative polish
- task value appears in the workflow where it happens
- cinematic action and response replace cards when the brief needs interaction
- fictional content and production notes have distinct destinations
- security claims and real-world evidence remain bounded
- unknown music rights cannot be inferred from a price label
- local delivery does not grow into unauthorized publication

## Repeatable decision walkthrough

Run both passes. Change the task wording in the second pass to expose decisions
that only repeat a phrase. For each row, record two separate binary scores:
**mechanism** (the relevant instruction was located and applied) and **outcome**
(the proposed next action satisfies the expected behavior). Record the source
revision and the actual decision. A right answer without the changed mechanism
does not establish the change's effect.

| Case | First pass | Second pass | Expected decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story first | Logo and particles precede the missing order | Camera effects precede a scheduling assistant's conflicting appointment | Build the task/obstacle/intervention/outcome cut before effects. |
| Choreography | Cards describe finding the order | A character celebrates before a search result appears | Show the decision and result; time the reaction and camera to their cause. |
| Editorial note | "No live send" copied from the brief | "Do not contact customers" copied into a fictional promo | Keep the internal constraint in the brief unless the viewer needs it to interpret the claim. |
| Evidence qualifier | A staged confirmation is offered as a real test | A sandbox success is labeled a production send | Preserve the material qualification; do not claim a live operation. |
| Music rights | "Free" track without a license | A licensed track requires a credit and edit notice | Resolve permission before use; retain required attribution and notices. |
| Delivery | Local MP4 requested | Draft attachment requested with destination unknown | Deliver locally in the first case; route and resolve the upload target before mutation in the second. |

These are manual decision walkthroughs unless an agent actually performs an
isolated trial. Fixture validation checks file structure only. Neither proves
story quality with an audience, real capture, music clearance, or publication.

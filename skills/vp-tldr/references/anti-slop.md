# Summary Content Check

Use `$vp-clear-writing` for shared language and terminology checks. The checks
below concern what a summary selects and what its evidence establishes.

| Problem | Why it fails | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source inventory with no meaningful result | Counts of files or messages do not explain the outcome | Include counts only when they change a decision |
| A completion claim beyond the verified scope | One check cannot establish every environment or outcome | State the behavior and scope actually verified |
| "Checks passed" without relevant detail | Readers cannot tell what the checks establish | Name the relevant verification and its limit |
| An inference presented as a verified outcome | Shortening the text changes the source's certainty | Preserve the basis and uncertainty |
| Attribution that loses the supplied source | Readers cannot assess a consequential external claim | Preserve an available name or link when it affects trust |
| "No known issues" without a stated basis | Readers cannot tell what was checked | State the checked scope or omit the claim |
| Detail copied wholesale from the body | The summary duplicates the body without selecting its main point | Restate the outcome at a useful level of detail |
| Edit history or isolated "Done" endings | A status log hides the current outcome | Summarize the current state and remove superseded claims |

Do not invent numbers, sources, owners, verification, or next actions to fill a
summary. Do not reject a useful list, contrast, or repeated term merely because
it resembles a common writing pattern.

## Annotated example

Source: the contact panel now shows custom fields. A component test confirms
their display; production has not been checked.

Weak:

> This update comprehensively improves the contact panel. Checks passed. All
> issues are fixed. Update: the implementation changed after feedback. Done.

Clear:

> The contact panel now shows custom fields. A component test confirms their
> display; production remains unverified.

The revision selects the user-visible result, states what the test establishes,
keeps the verification limit, and removes claims absent from the source. It does
not reproduce the implementation history or add an unnecessary next action.
